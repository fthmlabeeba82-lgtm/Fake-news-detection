About FakeCheck

Learn more about our Fake News Detection project.

What is FakeCheck?

FakeCheck is a web-based fake news detection system designed to help users analyze news articles and identify whether the information is likely to be real or fake. The project combines a user-friendly web interface with Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning techniques.

How Does It Work?

Users can submit a news article through the application. The submitted text will be processed by a Machine Learning model, which will analyze the content and provide a prediction along with a confidence value.
